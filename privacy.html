Privacy Policy

365 Days with God: Daily Bread

Effective date: November 18, 2026

In short: 365 Days with God does not ask you to create an account, does not collect your personal information, and does not show ads or track you. Your journal, streak, saved days and reminder settings are stored only on your own device.

This Privacy Policy explains how the 365 Days with God app (the “App”), published by BibleFix (“we,” “us”), handles information. It applies to the App on Android, on Google Play, and on the web at biblefix.github.io/365-withgod.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or sell personal information. The App has no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts, and everything you write in the App stays on your device.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the App work, some information is saved only in your device's local storage:

  • Your journal entries
  • Which days you have marked as read, and your reading streak
  • Days you have saved as favorites
  • Your reminder time and days

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. If you uninstall the App or clear your browser or app data, it is permanently deleted. We cannot recover it for you.

3. Notifications and calendar reminders

If you choose to turn on notifications, the App asks your device for permission and uses it only to remind you to read. If you use “Add reminder to my calendar,” the App creates a calendar file on your device that you can choose to add to your own calendar app. We do not access your calendar.

4. Third-party services

The App uses or links to a few outside services. When you use them, their own privacy policies apply:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the App's files. Like any website host, it may process technical information such as your IP address when the App's files are downloaded.
  • Google Fonts provides the App's typefaces. Your device may contact Google's servers to load them.
  • BibleGateway.com opens only if you tap “Read in NIV online.” Each day's passage is also built into the App (World English Bible) so you can read it offline.
  • PayPal opens if you choose to give through the Give page on the web version. Any payment is handled entirely by PayPal; we never see your card or account details.
  • YouTube (youtube.com) opens if you tap “Watch the latest video”. YouTube’s own privacy policy applies there.
  • Ko-fi (ko-fi.com) opens if you tap “Support me on Ko-fi” on the Give page or “Visit the BibleFix Shop” on the Shop page of the web version. Any payment is handled entirely by Ko-fi and its payment providers.

5. Advertising and analytics

The App contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

6. Children's privacy

The App is a devotional suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Data security

Because your information stays on your device, its security depends on your device. We recommend using a screen lock to protect your journal entries.

8. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact BibleFix at: biblefix316@gmail.com
